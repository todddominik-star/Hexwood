BUILT (through v8): D6 Hut & Old Stones (the "out" map), D5 The Old Road,
D4 Thistledown, D3 Willow Bend. (The hut interior, cellar and library
interior hang off these.)

AREAS (Todd's descriptions)
---------------------------
H6  #1: marker for a new area that will open up later, room to expand.
G1  Deep Caves: further into the heart of the Purple Mountains.
G2  Gold Mines: a cave into the Purple Mountains. Gold is possible, but it
    takes work.
G3  (not mapped)
G4  Cliff's Edge: a very small, rocky area next to the keep that might have a
    secret.
G5  The Blue Tower: a fairly fresh place, not overly run down. No plan yet.
G6  North Road: the way out of the current map.
F1  Dark Caves: more exploration within the Purple Mountains.
F2  Upper Purple Mountains: rocky area outside, high up the mountains.
F3  World's Edge: not literally, but the cliff edge to the right drops way
    down.
F4  (not mapped)
F5  Forest Path: the only way through the dark forest to the Blue Tower.
F6  (not mapped)
E1  (not mapped)
E2  Lower Purple Mountains: the lower rocky road and hillside leading up the
    impressive mountains.
E3  Lowlands: half grass, half rock approach to the mountains.
E4  Tig's Hallow: a clearing in the forest where Tig did something memorable,
    and the area earned the name.
E5  Dark Forest: thickly treed, little light.
E6  Western Forest: the dark forest continuing to the right.
E7  (not mapped)
D1  (not mapped)
D2  Running Water: a waterfall off the left side of the Purple Mountains
    creates the river that flows to Thistledown.
D3  Willow Road (built as Willow Bend).
D4  Thistledown (built).
D5  The Old Road (built).
D6  The Hut and the Old Stones: the original starting place (built).
D7  High Point: another cliff edge, reachable only from the south.
C1  (not mapped)
C2  Archery Yard: part of the keep grounds set aside for the dangerous sport.
C3  Sunny Lane: the once picturesque grassy approach to the Keep, from when
    it was a busy place.
C4  Crossroads: connects the Keep (via Sunny Lane), Thistledown, the Fairy
    Woods and the Quarry.
C5  Fairy Woods: a place of more magic than trees, but plenty of each.
C6  Reflection Pool: home of the water nymphs, and more magic.
C7  North Valley: cliff edge, and the only access to High Point.
B1  (not mapped)
B2  Labyrinth: full of thickets; the destination in the center is somewhere
    to go once, probably, for a reward or a further clue.
B3  THE KEEP: as laid out previously (see DEVNOTES: 5 levels, roof overview,
    Farsight / Locate / Carry spells).
B4  Western Side: grassy area next to the keep that may have hosted tents
    for visitors.
B5  Copper Quarry: copper.
B6  Granite Quarry: granite.
B7  Rainbow Crossing: a magical bridge leads off the cliff edge to the east.
B8  Sky Bridge: partway across the rainbow crossing; aerial engagement
    without the hero having to fly or float.
B9  #2: the rainbow road's destination, a place of high or advanced magic.
A1  (not mapped)
A2  Gazebo: once a relaxing garden with a large gazebo for Keep residents.
A3  Game Field: jousting, discus, javelin and other field games the Keep
    hosted long ago.
A4  Clay Swamp: a source of clay.
A5  Unknown, uncharted quarry area.
A6  Tin Quarry: tin.
A7  South Valley: the cliff continues south, creating the edge of the world.
