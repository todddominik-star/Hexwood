WHAT HEXWOOD IS
---------------
A calm, top-down exploration and puzzle adventure for the phone, played in the
browser. Zelda-style scrolling world, Myst-style curiosity, no combat.
No ads, no energy meter, no timers, no failure states. Long play sessions with save.

Inspirations: Zelda, Atari 2600 Adventure (level 3), Rogue, Banjo-Kazooie,
Donkey Kong Country (collecting, searching, power-ups), Myst (puzzles,
atmosphere), Marble Madness, the feel of R-Type / Tempest power-ups,
States Builder: Trade Empire (rebuilding / unlocking land).
No precise timing or pixel-exact taps needed to succeed.


THE HERO
--------
A spell-mapper who restores color to a faded, hexed land.
Eye for detail, interest in magic, loves puzzles.
Spells are tools, not weapons. Also collects magical curios.


CORE DECISIONS (locked)
-----------------------
View:      Top-down, world scrolls with the hero (like Zelda).
Controls:  Touch anywhere and slide to move (same as Void and Bloom).
Look:      Soft and colorful. (Aesthetic details get their own session.)
Rooms:     Hand-built, about a dozen per session. No generator for now.
Setting:   Starts in forest and meadow.

Color restoration:
  - The land starts gray.
  - When a puzzle or challenge is solved, that specific place goes gray -> color.

Creatures (no combat):
  - Hexed creatures block or wander. A spell un-hexes them into friendly
    critters (butterflies, bunnies, chipmunks, fireflies, etc.).
  - Some un-hexes give an instant reward.
  - Some freed critters beckon you to follow them to a reward.
  - Freeing certain groups opens a new door or path.

Secret chains:
  - Chains are random and secret. NO hint that one has started or is in progress.
  - Example: freeing butterflies is common and nice. Freeing 6 chipmunks makes
    them all come back and lead you to an opening that wasn't there before.
  - When a chain COMPLETES it announces itself with one or more of:
      chime, sparkle, a helper animal arriving from off-screen to help or lead,
      or a mystic note / cloud with a readable clue, e.g.
      "Take the trail toward the purple mountains, and after you pass the
       golden tree, take every right turn..."

Clue Journal:
  - Small, simple menu item (scroll icon).
  - Holds every clue received until it is resolved into its reward.
  - Resolved clues get checked off.

The Village:
  - Built from the creatures you un-hex: freed critters move in.
  - Rebuilding the village is a goal in itself (light layer, not a factory sim).
  - Materials gathered while exploring are used to rebuild.


THE OPENING: THE HUT
--------------------
  - Start inside a small hut. The hut is GRAY. Solving its puzzles brings it to
    full color (a two-step "splotchy" reveal is OK; once complete, fully colored).
  - A trapped fairy is in the hut. Freeing her is one of the first puzzles.
    She becomes the COACH: she asks for something out of her reach; handing it
    to her earns a hint about the next step. She also ENCHANTS ingredients.
  - A few short puzzles unlock a drawer/bureau holding the SPELLBOOK.
  - Spellbook page 1 lists ingredients for the first spell: WRITE.
  - Gather in the hut, assemble at the workbench, cast Write.
  - Casting Write writes the formula for spell #2.
  - After casting Write for spell #2, a puzzle/challenge leads to the KEY for
    the hut door. Then outside to explore and collect.


SPELL SYSTEM
------------
Loop: discover formula -> search for ingredients -> work them (grind, refine,
      polish, ask a fairy or nymph to enchant, etc.) -> cast.
  - WRITE: the first spell. Casting it costs a WISP FEATHER (somewhat rare) and
    writes the formula for the next spell into the book.
    Never more than 2-3 written-but-uncast formulas at a time. No stockpiling.
  - Wisp feathers are a typical secret-chain reward.
  - When a spell is first cast, its NAME is revealed and its first effects are
    written into the book (e.g. "makes light", "warms what the red circle rests
    on for 5 seconds").
  - Newly discovered effects are added to the spellbook AUTOMATICALLY. Recasting
    means going back to the book for the formula, so you notice the new notes.

Spell shapes MEAN something (at least 3 kinds, maybe more later):
  - Powder  = single use. Pull from the pouch, works once, gone.
  - Orb     = charged. Works N times, then needs a recharge.
  - Cube    = permanent enchantment cast on an object (e.g. Fire on the furnace:
              from then on, adding a few ingredients always makes fire for
              metal-working).

SEEKER ORB (secret finder):
  - Pull it out: you can't walk, but you turn in a circle where you stand and
    expose secret items/places within a few squares.
  - Secrets always have a TELL (shimmer, glow, a critter vanishing into a
    solid-looking place) so there's no "2 steps, orb, 2 steps, orb" habit.
  - 3 uses per charge. Upgrades: 3 -> 6 -> 9 and on, so you can range further.

RECHARGING:
  - Everything recharges at the hut.
  - Craftable WAYSTATIONS recharge some (not all) spells. Each waystation has its
    own set (the swamp one does these, the rocky-hillock one does others).
  - Idea from another game: a two-part TELEPORT spell. Cast "to hut" deep in the
    woods; it leaves a return marker. Do your things at the hut, then cast
    "return" to zap back to the marker.


CONTAINERS AND GEAR
-------------------
  - INGREDIENT SATCHEL: raw materials (feathers, jewels, ash...).
  - SPELL POUCH: finished spell items, ready to use.
  - Craftable gear: bags, pouches, better shoes, gloves with specific abilities,
    and the like. Gear can also be found in CHESTS.

WHAT'S BUILT (v1)
-----------------
Files: index.html (page + styles), js/data.js (items, spells, recipes, maps,
       objects, creatures), js/game.js (engine, rules, drawing, menus),
       manifest.json (add-to-home-screen).
Controls: touch anywhere and slide to walk. A purple button appears near
          things you can use. Top-left: Spellbook, Satchel, Pouch, Journal.
          (Keyboard on a computer: arrows/WASD, Space to act.)
Save: automatic, in the browser. Title screen has Continue / New Game.

The hut opening (all in):
  1. Wake up in the gray hut. Step stool under the bed -> reach the jar on
     the high shelf -> free FENNEL the fairy. Hut goes splotchy color.
  2. Fennel's requests (each earns a hint): honeycake crumb (cupboard),
     thimble of dew (windowsill), shiny button (sewing basket).
  3. Bureau dial lock: "From high to low, as light falls." Marks: moon on
     the high shelf, gold leaf in the painting, acorn on the rug.
     Answer: moon, leaf, acorn. Inside: Spellbook + first Wisp Feather.
  4. WRITE: ash (hearth) + berry ink (grind inkberries at the mortar) +
     goose quill (vase) at the workbench -> Write Powder. Cast from the
     Pouch, costs a Wisp Feather -> writes the LIGHT formula. Hut goes
     full color.
  5. LIGHT: candle stub (nightstand) + sunstone (clock pendulum) at the
     workbench -> Dim Orb -> Fennel enchants -> Light Orb (3 charges).
  6. Roll back the lumpy rug -> trapdoor -> dark cellar. With Light: door
     key on a hook, Wisp Feather in a crate.
  7. Unlock the door, go outside.

Outdoors (6 zones, each gray until restored):
  Hut Yard, Sunny Meadow, Willow Brook: free every hexed creature.
  Birch Grove: dark. Kindle its 3 lanterns while Light glows (this adds a
               new effect to the Light page automatically).
  The Old Stones: find the hidden Moon Pebble with the Seeker Orb.
  Chipmunk Hollow: secret, see the chain below.
SEEKER ORB: second Write (cellar feather) writes it. Shimmer petal (freed
  meadow butterfly, or the shimmering flower) ground at the mortar + river
  glass (brook bank) -> Clear Orb -> Fennel enchants. Standing still, it
  sweeps ~3.5 squares and reveals secrets. Secrets always shimmer.
Recharge orbs at the hut hearth.
Bunny (meadow) leads you to a Tiny Bell after being freed.
SECRET CHAIN: free all 6 chipmunks (yard, meadow x2, brook, grove,
  stones). No hint until the 6th. Then: chime, they gather around you, a
  note drifts down with a clue (saved in the Journal), they lead you to
  three leaning birches and vanish into the hedge. Seeker reveals the gap.
  Inside: Golden Acorn curio + Wisp Feather. Clue checks off when done.
Curios (Journal): Moon Pebble, Golden Acorn, Tiny Bell, Glass Marble
  (hidden in the yard).
A third Write casts but shows "more spells coming next version" (powder
  and feather are not used up).
